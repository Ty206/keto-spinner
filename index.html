      Why keto? 🥩

      The keto diet is a low-carbohydrate way of eating that focuses on protein, natural fats, and whole foods while limiting sugar and refined carbohydrates. Reducing refined carbs can help lower insulin levels, improve fullness, reduce cravings, and make it easier for the body to use stored fat for energy. Keto can also make fasting and eating fewer meals easier because protein and fat tend to keep you satisfied longer. The goal is not simply to eat less, but to choose foods that help control hunger, insulin, and overeating so weight loss becomes easier to maintain.

      Why fasting matters ⏰

      Fasting gives your body time to stop storing energy and start using stored body fat for fuel. When you eat, insulin rises and signals the body to store energy; when you fast, insulin falls, allowing stored fat to become more accessible. Regular fasting can also help reduce hunger, break the habit of constant eating, and improve control over cravings. The goal is not starvation—it is creating periods when the body can use the energy it has already stored instead of constantly receiving more food.
